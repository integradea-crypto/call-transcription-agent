# Call Analysis Rubric

Use transcript evidence to assess:

- request qualification: need, material, characteristics, volume, timing, delivery location, price, and selection criteria;
- decision process: contact role, decision maker, participants, approval path, and current supplier;
- manager behavior: discovery before presentation, relevant arguments, objection diagnosis, confirmation that the objection was resolved, and concise recap;
- next-step discipline: action, owner, date, and a customer commitment;
- recurring friction: objections, missing information, unclear ownership, and repeated delays.

For each finding, provide the call identifier, date, a short paraphrased evidence fragment, and confidence. Separate missing evidence from evidence that an action did not happen. A transcript may be incomplete or inaccurate, so avoid certainty where speaker attribution or wording is ambiguous.
